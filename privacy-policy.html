Privacy Policy - Quizmix

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how NVerse Games ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, shares, and retains data when you use Quizmix ("the Game"). By using the Game, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Collect

When you use Quizmix, we collect the following data through Epic Online Services:

  • Epic Games Account ID - a unique identifier for your account
  • Display Name - your Epic Games username
  • Language preference - your preferred language setting
  • Game progress data - including levels completed, in-game currency, and achievements
  • Online presence status - whether you are online or offline

3. How We Collect Data

Data is collected automatically through Epic Online Services when you:

  • Log in to the Game using your Epic Games account
  • Play the Game and make progress
  • Use online features such as lobbies and multiplayer
  • Save your game progress to cloud storage

4. How We Use Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • Account authentication - to verify your identity and allow you to log in
  • Save game progress - to store and sync your progress across devices
  • Online features - to enable multiplayer, lobbies, and friend features
  • Improve the Game - to understand how players use the Game and improve it
  • Customer support - to respond to your inquiries and resolve issues

5. How We Share Data

We share data with the following parties:

  • Epic Games - as the service provider for Epic Online Services, they process data on our behalf
  • Other players - your display name and online status may be visible to other players in multiplayer sessions

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

6. How We Retain Data

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. If you stop using the Game:

  • Game progress - stored in Epic Online Services cloud storage until you delete your account or request deletion
  • Account information - managed by Epic Games according to their privacy policy
  • Analytics data - anonymized and retained for up to 12 months

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Data is stored securely by Epic Online Services
  • We do not store passwords or sensitive information on our servers

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

9. Contact for Data Protection Inquiries

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us:

  • Email: nversegames.support@gmail.com
  • Response time: We aim to respond within 5 business days

10. Children's Privacy

Quizmix is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

12. Third-Party Services

Quizmix uses Epic Online Services for authentication, cloud saves, and multiplayer features. Epic Games handles data according to their own privacy policy at https://www.epicgames.com/site/en-US/privacypolicy.

By using Quizmix, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
